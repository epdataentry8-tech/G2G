FIB Card Delivery — Standard Operating Procedures

Managing the primary distribution pipeline for First Iraqi Bank requires precise execution. Below is the step-by-step workflow designed to process secure banking assets with zero operational variance.

1

2

10:00 AM — 11:00 AM
Manifest Reception & Ingestion
FIB transmits the daily data manifest. The back-office team monitors the intake. Delays past 11:00 AM trigger formal cross-organization escalations to preserve SLA timelines.
11:00 AM — 1:00 PM
Partner Portal Order Processing
Individual records are generated on the portal, assigning distinct tracking codes. These codes are reconciled and transmitted back to FIB on a daily cycle to ensure visibility.
1:00 PM — 2:00 PM
Secure Logistics Pickup & POP
Logistics personnel collect sealed batches from FIB, verifying item counts and securing transits with numbered security seals. This establishes the Proof of Pickup (POP).
2:00 PM — 4:00 PM
Custody Verification & Compliance Packaging
Batches are unsealed, counted, and verified against seal logs before the physical manifest is signed. Packaging protocols are then applied: branded materials for general zones and non-branded security packaging for specific regional requirements.
4:00 PM — End of Shift
Fulfillment Transfer
Every package is systematically labeled, scanned, and updated to "At Warehouse" status. This clean data handoff ensures downstream teams can execute last-mile fulfillment accurately.

⚡ Quality Control Milestone

Manifest delays past 12:00 PM require immediate intervention from the Back Office Team Leader. Past 1:00 PM, dispatch timelines are formally recalibrated to mitigate late-delivery risks.

Regulatory Compliance & Risk Management

Adherence to these core corporate policies guarantees data integrity, contract compliance, and security across all delivery channels.

Packaging Protocols

  • Branded EP nylon utilized for standard delivery zones
  • Non-branded white nylon mandatory for Sulaymaniyah and sensitive regions
  • Tamper-evident seals required on all active shipments
  • Deviations from packaging standards trigger formal QC incident reports

KYC & Identity Verification

  • Validation restricted to National IDs or Passports only
  • High-resolution digital IDs are acceptable upon strict clarity verification
  • ID photography must match the physical recipient
  • Third-party delivery (family or colleagues) is strictly prohibited

Documentation Audit Trail

  • Digital Daily Manifest directly from client database
  • Physical Card Handover Manifest with authorized signatures
  • Digital POD records containing signatures and ID verification photos
  • Reconciled RTS Manifests signed off by authorized bank representatives

Unsuccessful Delivery Protocol

  • Shipments are capped at a maximum of 3 delivery attempts
  • The Call Center initiates outreach following attempts 1 and 2
  • Address and contact updates must be logged prior to re-dispatch
  • Following the 3rd failed attempt, status moves to "For Return"
  • RTS inventory is isolated in restricted warehouse zones for up to 24 hours
  • Returned assets are handed back to client teams the next business morning

Zero-Tolerance Variances

  • Leaving secure financial assets unattended
  • Bypassing mandatory KYC identification checks
  • Storing corporate or client assets in unsecured environments
  • Disclosing customer information outside approved communication channels
  • Omitting systematic validation checkpoints
  • Accepting cash tips or payments on digital-wallet delivery batches

Institutional Commitment

Every asset handled by our department represents our clients' financial stability and institutional trust. The back office maintains a rigorous security posture, operating as a centralized control center. Through strict policy enforcement and internal audits, we safeguard our organization's professional standing and client relationships.
